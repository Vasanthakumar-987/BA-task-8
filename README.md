# BA-T8 — Business Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview

A Tableau dashboard showing sales by product line, deal size, year, and country.

## Visualizations

- **Treemap:** Sales contribution by product line and deal size.
- **Bar Chart:** Total sales by product line.
- **Line Chart:** Yearly sales trend from 2003 to 2005.
- **Filled Map:** Sales performance by country.

## Key Insights

- Classic Cars have the highest sales: **3,919,616**.
- Vintage Cars rank second: **1,903,151**.
- Trains have the lowest sales: **226,243**.
- Medium-sized Classic Cars deals form the largest treemap block.
- 2004 has the highest recorded sales among the displayed years.
